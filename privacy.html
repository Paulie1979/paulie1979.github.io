Pi Backups - Privacy policy

Pi Backups is a personal tool used only by its owner, Paul Graham.

It uses Google's "drive.file" permission, so it can only see and manage the backup files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot access any other files.

Backups are encrypted on the owner's Raspberry Pi before they are uploaded. No data is collected from anyone else, and nothing is shared with, sold to or used by any third party.
